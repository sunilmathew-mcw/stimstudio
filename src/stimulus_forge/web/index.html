StimStudio User Guide & How-To

Step-by-step workflow, features, and reference

How to Generate Stimuli (5 Easy Steps)

1
Enter or Load Seed Concepts

Type initial concepts (e.g. basketball, Michael Jordan) into the input box on the left, or click 📂 File… to import from CSV, Excel, TXT, Word (.docx), or questionnaire files. Set an optional Subject ID (e.g. SUBJ_TEST_001) to organize files per subject.

2
Choose Your AI Inference Provider

Click Settings at the top to select your provider:

  • Local Llama.cpp (GGUF): $0.00 cost, runs 100% locally on your Mac with zero network data transfer.
  • Cloud Models (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI): High-reasoning cloud inference with real-time token tracking.
3
Choose Image Source & Settings

Select where stimulus pictures come from:

  • Wikimedia & Wikipedia: Free, authentic high-res reference images.
  • DuckDuckGo / Bing: Web image search with strict safe-search filters.
  • Local Stable Diffusion / DALL-E: AI-generated images from scratch.
4
Generate & Curate the Concept Tree

Select whether you want Related concepts and/or Images, then click Generate. The system populates an interactive tree showing seed concepts and their associative expansions. Right-click any concept (or click the ➕ button) to type in custom related concepts in case generation missed a concept you need.

5
Inspect, Crop, or Export in Gallery

Click any thumbnail in the gallery to open the Lightbox. Inspect detected frontal faces and ROI bounding boxes, perform automatic 160×160 square face crops, or click the folder icon to reveal downloaded stimulus files on your computer.

Image Acquisition & Face Detection

👤 Face Detection & ROI

StimStudio automatically scans downloaded images for frontal faces and draws color-coded ROI (Region of Interest) bounding boxes. This ensures faces are centered, visible, and suitable for visual psychology/neuroscience paradigms.

✂️ Auto-Crop to Target Dimensions

Enable Auto-Crop in Settings (default: 160×160 px) to automatically center-crop and resize detected faces to uniform dimensions, eliminating tedious manual image editing for EEG/fMRI protocols.

📁 Subject-Specific Output Folders

Images are automatically organized into data/image_workspace/downloads/<Subject-ID>/<concept>/. You can click the folder icon on any gallery header to reveal the folder directly in Finder.

What is "FinOps Telemetry"?

FinOps (Financial Operations) is the discipline of tracking and managing cloud AI costs. In StimStudio, this is a passive monitoring tool so researchers don't accidentally burn through grant funding or cloud budgets.

⏱️ Latency (ms)

Measures the speed of generation calls (average and 95th percentile response times).

🔢 Tokens

Total prompt and completion tokens processed across all LLM requests.

💵 Spend ($)

Estimated dollar cost incurred from commercial cloud APIs based on official per-million token rates.

🛡️ Cost Avoided ($)

Dollars saved by utilizing free local models or the in-memory cache instead of cloud APIs.

💡 Pro-Tip: The Semantic LRU Cache prevents re-running identical prompts. Subsequent generations for the same seed return in under 2 ms at $0.00 cost!

Air-Gapped & HIPAA/IRB Compliance

When working with sensitive patient questionnaires or IRB-restricted concepts, you can run StimStudio in 100% offline mode:

  • Step 1: In Settings > Inference Provider, select Local Llama.cpp (GGUF). StimStudio will discover or let you browse for quantized .gguf model weights on your Mac.
  • Step 2: In Settings > Image Source, choose Local Stable Diffusion or use local folder image assets.
  • Result: Zero prompts, tokens, or images leave your local workstation. All computation executes locally on your CPU/Metal GPU with $0.00 cost and zero cloud transmission.
